There are three types variables
1. public :
  These variables can be accessed inside the class
  out side of the class.
  They can be Inheried
  self.variable
2. private:
   Private variables can be accessed with in the class
   They can't be accessed outside of the class
   They can't be Inhertied
   self.__variable

3. protected :
  protected members can be accessed with in the class
  they can be accessed outside of the class
  they can be inherited
  self._variablename
