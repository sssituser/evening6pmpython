append()	Adds one item
insert()	Adds item at a specific index
extend()	Adds multiple items

remove()	Removes a specific value
pop()	Removes an item by index
clear()	Removes all items

index()	Finds the index of an item
count()	Counts occurrences
sort()	Sorts the list
reverse()	Reverses the list


[start:count:step]===> Insertion order
[step:End:start]===> Reverse order
